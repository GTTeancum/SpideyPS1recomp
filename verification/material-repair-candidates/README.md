# Staged Material Repairs

Gwenom and Venom were reconverted with the current per-material pipeline into
the task's `work/repair-initial-materials/suits` directory. These candidates are
not installed at the time these candidate reports were recorded. Both authored
slots and all source triangles survive.

Both candidates passed full structural and 4196-frame animation checks,
115-frame web checks, exact fallback texture checks and finger contraction
checks. The managed CustomSuitModelRegression loader accepted both candidates
and their material mappings. Venom's head-relative tongue attachment also
passed all frames. These results do not accept fist appearance or tongue pose.

The candidates have since passed individual native gameplay review and were
installed after hash checks and full backups. Current installation evidence is
in `verification/repair-initial-materials`; these reports retain the staging
history rather than representing an outstanding installation task.
